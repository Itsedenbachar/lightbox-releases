Moodlib
macOS 15 or later

A place to keep what things should look like.

Moodlib is a field of film stills, photos and clips for filmmakers. Drop references in, pan through them, pull them onto boards. A local AI tags them so you can search by light, mood and place.

Download for Mac iPad TestFlight soon

Free while in development. Updates arrive inside the app. Your library syncs between Mac and iPad over iCloud.

Search that understands pictures

Type “warm kitchen at night” or “wide shot, overcast beach”. The matching runs on your Mac, so nothing leaves your library.

Stills from any video

Paste a YouTube or Vimeo link and grab the exact frame you mean, at the stream’s full sharpness.

Yours, on disk

The library is a folder of files you own. Sync it to the iPad over iCloud, draw on it with the Pencil, and have it back on the Mac.
